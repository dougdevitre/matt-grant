## Bio

Matt's story runs from three public schools to a legal career and now to public service. In his early years he earned the rank of **Eagle Scout** with the Boy Scouts of America. The arc of his life — education, the law, and now a run for Congress — is grounded in a consistent set of values: **families, fair justice, honesty, service, and opportunity for all.**
