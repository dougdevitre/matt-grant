## Why these targets

mo-gov maps the **Judiciary and Civil and Criminal Jurisprudence** and **Families, Seniors and Health**
committees as Tier-1 for "family law, custody, court documentation." That is precisely the terrain of
Matt's CHILD Protection Act. Members of these committees are the Missouri officials most likely to (a)
share the family-court-reform message, (b) lend a credible endorsement on the issue, and (c) amplify it
in-state.
